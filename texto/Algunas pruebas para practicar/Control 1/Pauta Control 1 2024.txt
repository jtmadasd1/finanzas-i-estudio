b) Based on your previous answer in this question, how much should an agent invest in the risky 
Market portfolio and in a risk-free bank deposit? The agent has mean-variance preferences that 
are described by equation (1): 
U(E[Rp], 2p) = E[Rp] - 2p                  (1)          
where p is a portfolio composed of a risk-free asset and a risky asset. 
Assume that: 2M = (16%)2 = 0.0256. [30pts] 
Hint: Remember that an investor whose mean-variance preferences are: 
 U(E[Rp], 2p) = E[Rp] – (c/2)2p 
That investor will invest a proportion of their wealth, α, in the risky asset, R, equal to: α= (risk 
premium)/(c*2R) 
Answer 
 
In our case, it is clear that c=2, so: α = (E[RM] - rf)/[22M],  
 
Given the results from the previous part and the fact that: M = 0.16, then: 
 
 α = (0.20)/(2*0.0256) = 3.91,  
 
which represents 391% of the investor's wealth. This means that the investor needs to borrow 
from the bank up to 291% of their initial investment capital.
