Question II.  
2a) Option prices are calculated using a risk neutral valuation, by assuming that all agents in the 
economy have a null risk aversion. For that reason, it is possible to directly discount options' 
payoffs at the risk-free rate. Please, explain potential problems of assuming a risk neutral 
valuation in which all agents do not have risk aversion.  
Answer 
 
This sentence is wrong. The risk neutral valuation does not assume that all agents in the 
economy have a null risk aversion. In fact, agents can have different levels of risk aversion. 
 
The risk neutral valuation is a technique, in which the risk is eliminated by using a portfolio. 
Thus, the concept 'risk neutral valuation' is related to eliminate risk rather than assuming a null 
level of risk aversion in the economy. This risk neutral portfolio has the option contract and a 
quantity of stock shares. After that, the payoffs are discounted to the present at the risk free rate 
since this portfolio does not have risk. Then, it is possible to obtain the price of the option by 
decomposing the portfolio in its different components. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2b) Why should students in Chile learn 'options' despite there are no developed option markets 
in Chile? Provide one reason. 
Answer 
 
-All insurances are options, and there are a plenty of insurance companies in Chile (e.g., Isapres 
and insurance companies related to car insurances, travel insurances, etc.). Thus, students in 
Chile in this area will face with option modelling and option pricing. 
 
or 
 
-Options can be replicated (in a synthetic way) by using a bond and stock; thus it is not 
necessary to have an option market to use them.
