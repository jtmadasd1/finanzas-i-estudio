Question II:  
2a) Please, provide an example when two individuals (for instance, agent I and agent II) can 
have different beliefs, even if they receive the same type of information. 
Answer:  
 
Two individuals can process the information in a different way in their minds (or even in their 
computers). For instance, the agent I can calculate the expected returns of an asset as the 
average daily returns of the last month (i.e. she/he doesn't have a long memory); while the agent 
II can calculate the expected returns of an asset as the average daily returns of the last 10 years 
(i.e. agent II has loner memory than agent I).  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2b) Why the market value of a company can be different from what is reflected in its accounting 
book (i.e. the company accounting used, for example, to pay taxes). 
Answer:  
 
The company accounting reflects only what the company effectively has received from its 
business. However, the market value of the same company also reflects the potential 'future' 
projects (and their cash flows), which are not received yet.
