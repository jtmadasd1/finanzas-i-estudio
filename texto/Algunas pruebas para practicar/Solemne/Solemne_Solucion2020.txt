Question II:  
2a) Camila has received an email from Margarita, with an investment opportunity where Camila 
can get a huge expected return of 30000%.  Camila thought that was a lie, and she went to the 
police station. In the police station, the police officer (who has some knowledge in finance) 
explains to her that it was true. Why?  
Answer:  
 
It is true, because the investment opportunity from Margarita involves a huge level of leverage, 
which increases risk. Actually, you can have an investment opportunity with an infinite expected 
return, if there is an infinite level of risk (which can be reached with an infinite level of leverage). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2b) In financial markets, we discussed in class that individuals can be classified as 'patient' and 
'impatient'. Let's focus, in this question, only on 'patient' individuals. We learned that 'patient' 
individuals (who are waiting for the arrival of 'impatient' guys to perform a transaction) can 
have different levels of patience. Please, provide a concrete-real example, where two individuals 
(for instance, agent A and agent B, both classified a 'patient' individuals) has different levels of 
patience. 
Answer: 
 
Suppose agent A and agent B are selling avocados in the fruit market. Both agents are waiting for 
impatient individual who want to buy avocados as soon as possible (because impatient 
individuals do not want to wait for a potential future, but cheaper, delivery of avocados). 
 
Agent A can have a lower level of patience of agent B to sell avocados, if Agent A received her/his 
avocados earlier than agent B. Thus, the avocados of agent A are closer to be rotten than agent B. 
Then, Agent A can have a lower level of patience of agent B to sell the avocados.

The returns are affected by liquidity costs given by the market microstructure.

the effect of spread (immediacy costs) on return which is inversely related to investors’ holding 
period  .  
 
This liquidity cost is reflected in an additional 'premium' (i.e. a liquidity premium) that is paid by 
investors.
