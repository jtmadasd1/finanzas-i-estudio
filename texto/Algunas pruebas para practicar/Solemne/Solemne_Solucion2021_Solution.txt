Question II:  
2a) The Central Bank of Each country provides interest rates; and thus we can price bonds and 
other financial instruments related to interest rates. Please, explain this sentence. 
Answer: 
 
It is the opposite. We observe bond prices, and then we obtain implicitly interest rates.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2b) The interest rates for bonds from Central banks are practically constant (over time and with 
different maturities) since they have the support of the complete government of a country, and 
thus there are almost risk free. Please, explain this sentence. 
Answer: 
 
Interest rates change over time due to news related to credit risk (default risk), waiting cost 
(willingness of postponing consumption), and competition between agents who want to lend. 
 
In addition, interest rates change depending of the maturity, since the elements described above 
are also different depending the future repayment dates.
