Question II:  
2a) Please, provide an example when the curve of interest rates of government bonds (of a given 
country) decreases as the debt-maturity goes up. 
Answer: 
 
When in the short-term there is high uncertainty, which could affect the short-term default risk, 
but the agents in the economy know that such uncertainty will disappear in the future (e.g. 
presidential elections). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2b) In question 1 of the homework: What was the effect on the mean-variance efficient frontier 
of the additional investment constraints (e.g. short-sales constraints or investment limits)? 
Answer 
 
The mean-variance efficient frontier shows a different shape as the one presented in class due to 
the additional investment constraints. Actually, it is kind of a “mini” mean-variance frontier, 
since it is inside the unconstrained one.
